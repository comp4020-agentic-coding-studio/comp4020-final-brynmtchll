1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was the redesign in 9c6113c. Until then I had a chat room that happened to show tone. I saw clearly that the app should be a live stage: a spot for each speaker, words appearing as they're said, nothing kept as a log. Once I had that picture, every decision got easier, because I could ask whether it served the stage. It also told me what to cut, and I cut a lot.

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to be the developer who holds a clear vision and refuses to let the tool dilute it. Claude could build almost anything I described, so the hard part was knowing what to describe, and what to delete. I also learned to trust tests on real data over how a demo looks. The 178 acted takes showed me things I couldn't have seen by eye, like the unreliable "faster/slower" label. I want to keep working this way: a sharp spec, evidence from real use, and the willingness to throw away working things that don't serve the vision.
